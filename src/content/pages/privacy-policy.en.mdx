## Photo Privacy

<span id="photo-privacy"></span>

wywmysweetie AI needs two portrait photos so we can generate your AI trend video (typically you + a loved one). This section covers those images specifically.

### What photos are uploaded

You upload two images for face/reference-based video generation. Photos must be ones you have the right to use. Adults 18+ only.

### How we process photos

- On Create, your browser sends the images to our generation API (typically as temporary `data:` image payloads, or as https image URLs if you provide those).
- We forward those images to **third-party AI video generation providers** so they can produce your video. Providers may change as we operate the service.
- We **do not sell** your photos.
- We **do not use your photos to train publicly marketed models**. This codebase has no photo training pipeline; photos are used only to fulfill your generation request.

### Retention (what the product actually does)

- **Our servers:** Uploaded portrait photos are **not saved as durable files in our database or video storage**. Generation task records store metadata (effect, resolution, status, credits) — not a retained copy of your upload for later reuse on our side.
- **Your browser:** A temporary draft may stay in **session storage on your device** so checkout return or a busy-provider retry can resume without re-uploading. That draft lives only in your browser until you clear site data, finish/clear the draft, or the session ends.
- **Third-party AI providers:** Providers receive the images to run generation and may keep operational copies under **their own** retention rules for a limited time. We cannot promise a provider-side delete timestamp beyond asking them to process only what is needed to deliver your video.
- **Generated videos:** Finished videos may be stored in our video storage so you can download them later. That is the output file, not your original upload archive.

If we later add server-side upload archiving, we will update this section with a concrete retention window before relying on that behavior.
