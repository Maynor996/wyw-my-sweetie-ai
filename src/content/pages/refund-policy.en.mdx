How credit packs, failed renders, and refunds work on **wywmysweetie AI** (wyw-my-sweetie.chatgpt-plus.workers.dev).

## Automatic credit returns

If a render fails — an error, a safety-filter refusal, a timeout, or a finished video withheld by our safety check — the credits it used are **returned automatically**. You do not need to contact us. A render that completes successfully but that you don't like is not a failed render.

## Credit packs

wywmysweetie AI sells one-time credit packs. Credits are used when you generate a video. Pack credits do not expire unless a pack page says otherwise.

## Refund requests

- **Unused packs:** request a full refund within **7 days** of purchase if you have not used any credits from the pack.
- **Partially used packs:** within the same **7 days**, unused credits may be refunded at that pack's per-credit price. Used credits are not refundable.
- **After 7 days**, or for credits already used to render a video, purchases are final.
- **Billing errors / duplicates:** open Settings → Support tickets within 30 days with the order number and we will correct it promptly.

## How to request

Open a support ticket from **Settings → Support tickets** and include your order number (Settings → Payments). Approved refunds go back to your original payment method through Stripe and usually arrive within 5–10 business days.

## Chargebacks

Please contact us before you file a chargeback. We can usually fix the problem faster. Fraudulent chargebacks (disputing charges for credits you used) may lead to account suspension.

## Statutory rights

Nothing in this policy limits refund rights that mandatory consumer law grants you in your country of residence.
