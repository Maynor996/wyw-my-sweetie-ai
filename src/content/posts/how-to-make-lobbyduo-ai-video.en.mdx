LobbyDuo turns one or two photos into a short hotel lobby duo dance clip. You can start on [tryhotellobby.app](https://tryhotellobby.app) without a long signup flow.

## What you need

- 1–2 clear front-facing adult photos you own or have permission to use
- About a minute to upload and pay
- A phone or laptop browser

Do not upload children’s photos or faces you do not have rights to use.

## Steps

1. Open [https://tryhotellobby.app](https://tryhotellobby.app).
2. Upload Photo 1 (and Photo 2 if the template supports a second person).
3. Keep the on-page aspect / resolution presets (usually 720p).
4. Press **Create**. If credits are low, Stripe Checkout opens — guests can pay with a real email.
5. Wait for generation, then download the MP4.

## Pricing

One-time packs start around **$8.88** per video equivalent. No subscription. See Pricing on the site for current packs.

## Tips

- Face fills most of the frame; soft light; no heavy sunglasses
- One person per photo works best
- Sister tools: [tools page](https://tryhotellobby.app/tools)
