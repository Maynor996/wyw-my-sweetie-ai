Face quality decides most of the result. The motion template locks choreography; the model mainly needs a clean identity lock.

## Framing

- Head and upper shoulders fill the frame
- Eyes roughly camera-level
- Leave a little space above the hair

## Lighting

- Soft daylight beats neon and heavy shadows
- Sharp JPEG/PNG; avoid beauty blur and tiny screenshots
- One face only — group photos confuse the lead

## Rights

Only use faces you may process. No minors. For marketing samples prefer licensed or public portraits.
